Privacy notice

MV Nzama Dental respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

If you use our booking form we collect your name, phone number, email address (optional), preferred date and time, reason for your visit, and any notes you add.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about your appointment request and to arrange your visit. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

Who sees it

  • Our practice staff.
  • Our form provider (FormSubmit), which passes your request to our email. If you choose the WhatsApp or Email buttons instead, your message goes through those services.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us on or . If you have a complaint you can contact the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Health information

Please don't put detailed medical information in the booking form. Share that with us in person at your consultation.
